Privacy policy

Bore & Bury's internal operations tool connects to one Google account, the company's own, and uses a single permission: sending email (the Gmail "send" permission).

  • The tool only sends messages, such as approval codes and status alerts, from that account to the company itself.
  • It does not read, store or delete any email.
  • It collects no information from the public or from any other Google user, and shares no data with anyone.
  • The tool is not available to anyone outside Bore & Bury.
